About Srivari Constructions

Trusted Builders Crafting Tomorrow's Landmarks

Founded in 1990 by Mr. Munuswamy, Srivari Constructions has grown into one of Tamil Nadu’s trusted construction companies with over three generations of excellence in civil engineering and infrastructure development.

Under the leadership of Mr. Thirusangu and Managing Partner & CEO T. Muhilvannan B.E., the company has earned the trust of clients and successfully delivered numerous residential, commercial, industrial, road, bridge, and government projects across Tamil Nadu.
