FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a quote for my construction project?

Simply fill out our contact form above or call us at +91 90030 80565. We offer free initial consultations. Our team will visit your site, understand your requirements, and provide a detailed quotation within 3–5 working days.

What areas do you serve?

Our primary service area is Trichy (Tiruchirapalli) and surrounding districts. We also undertake projects in Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, and other parts of Tamil Nadu for larger commercial and infrastructure projects.

What types of construction projects do you handle?

We handle all types — residential (homes, apartments, villas), commercial (offices, hotels, malls), industrial (warehouses, factories), infrastructure (roads, bridges), interior works, and renovation projects of all scales.

Do you provide post-construction warranty?

Yes. We provide a structural warranty on all new construction projects as per RERA guidelines, along with a workmanship warranty on finishing and fitouts. Details vary by project type and scope — these are clearly specified in the contract.

How long does a typical construction project take?

Timeline depends on project scope. A typical 3 BHK home takes 12–18 months. Commercial projects vary from 18 months to 3+ years. We provide a detailed project schedule at the outset and are committed to meeting the agreed deadlines.
