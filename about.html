Who We Are

Srivari Constructions Building Trust Since 1990

Founded in 1990 by Mr. Munuswamy, Srivari Constructions has grown into one of Tamil Nadu’s trusted construction companies with over three generations of excellence in civil engineering and infrastructure development.

Under the leadership of Mr. Thirusangu and Managing Partner & CEO T. Muhilvannan B.E., the company has successfully delivered numerous residential, commercial, industrial, road, bridge, and government projects across the state.

Our Journey

Company History

From a small contractor to Trichy’s premier construction company — our journey of growth and excellence.

2009

Foundation Year

Srivari Constructions was founded by T. Muhilvannan B.E. in Trichy, Tamil Nadu. Started with residential construction projects in Ponnagar and surrounding areas.

2012

First Commercial Project

Successfully completed the first large-scale commercial project ” a multi-story office complex in Anna Nagar, Trichy. This milestone opened doors to the commercial construction segment.

2015

100 Projects Milestone

Crossed the 100-projects milestone with a diversified portfolio spanning residential, commercial, and institutional sectors. Team expanded to 50+ skilled professionals.

2017

Infrastructure Division

Launched a dedicated Civil Infrastructure division handling road construction, bridges, and government contracts. Secured first government infrastructure project worth 25 Crore.

2020

Expansion Across Tamil Nadu

Expanded operations beyond Trichy to Chennai, Madurai, and Coimbatore. Introduced advanced construction technologies and adopted BIM (Building Information Modeling).

2024

500+ Projects & 500Cr+ Worth

Celebrating 15 years of excellence with 500+ completed projects totaling over 500 Crore in value. Recognised as one of Trichy's most trusted construction companies.
